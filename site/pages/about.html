About this project

I'm Luca Murri, a Master in Management candidate at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business (Class of 2027).

My background is in pharmaceutical marketing. My thesis compares pricing and reimbursement strategies in Europe and the United States. I'm interested in consulting, pharma strategy and the intersection of healthcare and AI.

The Pharma Access Atlas grew out of a simple question from that research: when a new drug is “approved”, how long does it take before patients can actually get it, and on what terms? Public sources answer that question in pieces, across four agencies and three languages. This project puts the pieces side by side, with a source behind every number.

How it was built

The data pipeline (Python) and the site (plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript) were built with Claude Code as an AI-augmented workflow. I set the scope, the data rules and the analysis; the AI helped write code and gather sources, and every researched value carries a link so it can be checked. The site is static, free to host, and refreshes its automatable data monthly.
