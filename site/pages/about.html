About this project

I'm Luca Murri, a Master in Management candidate at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business (Class of 2027).

My background is in AI and digital transformation consulting, and in product management and business development in pharma. I'm interested in pharma strategy and the intersection of healthcare and AI.

Where it started

The atlas began with my bachelor's thesis at LUISS Guido Carli in Rome, Pricing and Reimbursement Strategies in the Pharmaceutical Industry: Strategic Analysis of Europe and the United States (2025, supervised by Prof. Giuseppe Maria Bifulco). It compares the largely free-pricing US market with Europe's regulated systems: health technology assessment, reference pricing, confidential discounts, and the delays they create.

The thesis reviews policy and published studies. I kept running into a question those sources answer only through averages: when a new drug is “approved”, how long does it take before patients can actually get it, and on what terms? The answer for each drug is public, but it is split across seven agencies and four languages. This project puts the pieces side by side, with a source behind every number.

I finished the thesis in September 2025 and haven't updated it since. Where its figures differ from this site's, the site has the more recent, fully sourced data.

How it was built

A Python pipeline pulls data from public APIs and official files, and the site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Values no free API provides were researched from official pages, and each one carries a link so it can be checked. The site is static, free to host, and refreshes its automatable data monthly.
