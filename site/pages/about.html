About this project

I'm Luca Murri, a Master in Management candidate at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business (Class of 2027).

My background is in pharmaceutical marketing. My thesis compares pricing and reimbursement strategies in Europe and the United States. I'm interested in consulting, pharma strategy and the intersection of healthcare and AI.

The Pharma Access Atlas grew out of a simple question from that research: when a new drug is “approved”, how long does it take before patients can actually get it, and on what terms? Public sources answer that question in pieces, across seven agencies and four languages. This project puts the pieces side by side, with a source behind every number.

How it was built

A Python pipeline pulls data from public APIs and official files, and the site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Values no free API provides were researched from official pages, and each one carries a link so it can be checked. The site is static, free to host, and refreshes its automatable data monthly.
